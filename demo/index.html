What to look for

Four things on every one of these pages that most athletes don't have

01

A media kit a brand can read in ten seconds

Reach, engagement rate, audience split and a contact route, published and current. No sponsor has to ask, which means the athlete isn't negotiating from behind.

02

A signup form above the fold

Followers are rented. This is the one asset on the page that survives an account ban, a transfer and a retirement.

03

Somewhere to spend money

Merch that doesn't exist until it's paid for, plus bookable clinics and appearances. Goodwill with nowhere to go is goodwill nobody gets paid for.

04

A named sponsor slot

Logos on the page, and a slot in every email that goes out. It's the thing you show a sponsor when you ask about next season.
