The honest comparison

Everybody else takes a cut. We don't.

This is the whole difference, so it's worth being blunt about it. Here's what the people around an athlete's brand typically charge, and what they actually do for it.

Ascendr£39 to £1,999/moA flat fee that matches where you are in your career, everything registered in your name. Zero percent of your wages, your sponsorships, your appearances or your shop.

If we ever start taking a percentage, the best thing about us is gone — so we won't. No revenue share, no cut of a deal, and absolutely no equity in a young athlete's future earnings.

And to be clear about where the line is: we handle brand, content and sponsorship. We do not negotiate playing contracts or transfers. That is licensed work under FA and FIFA agent regulations, and it's your agent's job rather than ours.
