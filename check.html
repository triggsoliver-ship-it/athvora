What we look at

Six pillars, and why
each one costs you money

01

Do you exist?

A searchable name and a domain you control — or just profiles on platforms that could change the rules tomorrow. If a sponsor's first search lands on someone else's website, you don't own your own front door.

02

The media kit test

Reach, engagement, audience split, contact route and a rate. A marketing manager with a budget and a deadline gives up after two minutes and calls someone easier.

03

Consistency

Whether your rhythm survives a bad run of form, an injury or a loan spell — because the gaps are exactly when an audience decides whether it stays.

04

Engagement quality

Replies and saves against the benchmark for your follower count. Brands stopped buying follower numbers a long time ago.

05

Owned audience

An email list on your own domain. This is almost always the lowest score in the report, and it's the one that matters most the day an account disappears.

06

Commercial doors

A shop, a booking link, an enquiry form. Goodwill with nowhere to go is goodwill you never get paid for.
