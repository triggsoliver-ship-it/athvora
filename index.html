The uncomfortable maths

You get about eight years of everyone knowing your name. Then you get fifty years of the rest of your life.

Every athlete knows this. Almost nobody acts on it — because acting on it means doing marketing, and marketing feels like either vanity or a second job you didn't sign up for.

So the advice everyone gets is post more. But followers are rented. The algorithm is the landlord, and it can put your rent up or evict you without telling you. The day you stop performing, the reach goes, and there is nothing left to show for eight years of attention.

Ascendr turns rented attention into things you actually own. A domain. An email list. A product line. A customer base. Assets that survive a transfer, an injury and a retirement — and are still yours at thirty-four, when the football stops.
